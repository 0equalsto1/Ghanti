Privacy Policy for OmniClock

Last updated: November 18, 2026

OmniClock ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Use

OmniClock is a local utility application. We do not collect, transmit, store on remote servers, or share any personal information. All alarm schedules, world clock selections, memos, and settings are stored locally on your device.

2. Device Permissions Used

  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM: Used solely to trigger your alarms and reminders at the exact time you schedule.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used to display alarm alerts, snooze controls, and ongoing alarm banners.
  • VIBRATE: Used to vibrate your device when an alarm rings.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Used to restore your active alarms automatically if your device restarts.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE: Used to ensure the alarm audio and notification persist until you snooze or dismiss it.

3. Third-Party Services

OmniClock does not integrate third-party analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising networks.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via our GitHub repository or contact email.
